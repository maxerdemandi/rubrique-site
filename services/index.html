From soft goals to hard evidence

Every soft claim can become a measure.

High-impact, student-centered programs generate real outcomes, but they live outside the assessment machinery built for degree programs. Their goals are usually written in soft language: students will feel prepared, gain global competence, build belonging.

Left there, those goals cannot be defended to a provost or a board. But every soft claim your programs make can become an impact or return-on-investment measure. Rubrique Advisory Co. translates them into measurable indicators, evaluates the program against the established conditions and benchmarks research ties to real gains, and connects the results to broader institutional objectives like enrollment, retention, giving, and graduate earnings.

The flagship

The Signature Program Evaluation

Our end-to-end flagship program evaluation includes all phases below: we define the outcomes that matter, test the program against the eight conditions that make high-impact practices work, measure results against a defensible design, tie them to the outcomes your institution acts on, and produce the stakeholder-ready narrative for recruitment, advancement, and the board.

If you are not ready to commit to the full assessment cycle or have a more targeted need, each phase is also available as a smaller package.

Phase Zero Data Readiness Diagnostic

The first step.

A tailored, fixed-scope, fixed-fee engagement (roughly 6 to 8 weeks) that examines what data you already have, how it flows and connects, what it measures, where the gaps sit, and what questions it can answer. We also help you define quantifiable, measurable success metrics. Best for any institution that wants a low-risk first step toward understanding its high-impact experiences through an evidence-driven lens before committing to larger work.

You get:

  • A written data diagnostic report
  • An organized, clearly labeled data repository with defined relationships
  • A prioritized roadmap for future assessment work
  • A plain-language catalog of the questions your data can and cannot yet answer

Phase One Strategy & Measurement Design

Get the measurement right before the program starts.

We set success metrics with your program leaders, design the data-collection and analysis plan, and build the standards and workflows that keep the data usable later. Work can include counterfactual and equity-of-access analysis design, historical baselines, and pre-mortem planning.

You get:

  • A measurement strategy tied to program and institutional goals
  • Validated instruments, such as surveys and interview protocols
  • A full data-collection and analysis plan your team can run

Best for new program cycles, or any effort being built without a measurement plan.

Phase Two Experience Monitoring & Data Quality

Keep it on track while it runs.

We carry out live data collection, track the agreed metrics, protect data quality, and keep the work in line with institutional, state, federal, and accreditation requirements. We build internal-facing, external-facing, or combined dashboards your own people can read, with check-ins timed to the program rather than a fixed midpoint.

You get:

  • A live monitoring system and dashboards
  • A regular reporting cadence
  • Ongoing data-quality safeguards

Best for programs in flight that need to protect quality and show progress. Can be added on top of Phase One.

Phase Three Impact & Story

Prove it, then tell it.

We measure impact against the metrics set in Phase One, run the analyses the design allows (from descriptive comparisons and hypothesis tests to A/B and quasi-experimental designs), and write the narrative that turns outcomes into something trustees, accreditors, donors, and campus leaders will act on, aligned with how your institution talks about itself elsewhere.

You get:

  • A full impact analysis report
  • A communication suite: social content, a press release, and website copy
  • A post-mortem on what worked and what to improve

Best for end-of-cycle evaluation, accreditation evidence, and the fundraising case.
