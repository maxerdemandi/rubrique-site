Degrees have converged

  • Degrees have converged.
  • Course offerings are standardized across institutions.
  • Programs are hard to distinguish on paper.
  • Schools invest in their high-impact programs now more than ever.

What separates institutions is the experience they build around the degree. The programs that carry your identity are the least measured and evaluated operations on your campus. Rubrique Advisory Co. conducts a full, evidence-based assessment of these programs, ties them to your institutional goals and outcomes (e.g., employment and earnings outcomes; donor and alumni relations; recruitment, matriculation, and retention), and writes the stories for your key audiences: campus community, trustees, donors, prospective students and families, and oversight and accreditation agencies.

Assessment shops hand over a dashboard and stop. Communications shops write a glossy story without owning the numbers. We do both, so the story never outruns the evidence and the evidence never dies in a spreadsheet.

How we work

Four steps, one team.

01

Strategize

We work with you to understand your institutional goals and priorities, then design a bespoke data, analysis, and assessment strategy that answers the questions your organization wants answered.

02

Measure

We design the metrics and instruments, then collect and analyze the data, from a straightforward comparison to advanced modeling and simulation. The method fits the question, not the other way around.

03

Interpret

We tell you what the numbers mean for your budget and your goals: where to spend, where to cut, and what to protect. Plain enough for a board, rigorous enough to survive scrutiny.

04

Communicate

We turn the result into a narrative your stakeholders will act on (trustees, faculty, donors, prospective families, and regulators), kept consistent with how your institution already talks about itself.

Why now?

Differentiation has moved off the transcript and onto the holistic campus experience, where institutions can least prove it. Federal policy now ties program-level employment and earnings outcomes to funding, making return on investment a survival question. Colleges are shifting from growth by expanding program selection and class sizes to growth by choosing what to keep, cut, and rebuild, and those calls need evidence-based assessment and communication to campus leaders. The institutions that do well will be the ones that can prove their value and tell that story clearly.
